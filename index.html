Help & FAQ
How do I add money (deposit)?

Open Deposit, choose a service (bKash, Nagad, Rocket and others), a bank, or Crypto. Send the money to the number or address shown, then enter the amount, the transaction ID and a screenshot of your payment. Your balance is added after the payment is checked.

How long does a deposit take?

Mobile and bank deposits are normally checked within 15 minutes. If yours is still pending after 1 hour, contact Support with your transaction ID. Crypto deposits are added after we confirm the transfer.

How do I withdraw?

Open Withdraw, choose a method, enter your account number and the amount. To withdraw you need at least one completed deposit and one product purchase. The minimum is ৳1,000. The fee is shown before you submit.

My withdrawal is not paid yet.

Withdrawals are paid by our agents. After an agent accepts, they pay within 1 hour, otherwise another agent takes it. Check Transactions for the status. If you did not receive the money, tap "I did not receive" on the Home page or contact Support.

How do orders and delivery work?

Pay for a product from your wallet balance. You can then take the product (delivery) or sell it back to the company. Delivery takes 12-15 days and the delivery charge is ৳170.

How does buyback work?

If you sell back after the maturity days shown on the product, you get the price you paid plus the company percentage. If you sell before maturity you get 60% of the price you paid.

How do referrals work?

Share your referral link. A friend counts when they sign up, and your commission is added after their valid product purchases reach ৳1,000. The commission rate is set by the company.

Do I need KYC?

KYC is optional. You can buy, sell back, deposit and withdraw without it.

I forgot my password.

On the sign in screen tap Forgot password. Accounts created with a phone number are reset through Telegram.

How do I contact you?

Open Support Center. You can chat with the company or message us on Telegram @aprp_support. These are our only official contacts.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

APRP Digital ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

Account details (email or phone number, username, password in protected form); wallet and transaction records; deposit and withdrawal details you enter (sender account, transaction ID, payment screenshots); order and delivery details (name, phone, address); referral records; support messages; and, only if you choose KYC, the identity documents you upload. If you use our Telegram bot, your Telegram ID is linked to your account.

Why we use it

To run your wallet, check and process deposits and withdrawals, deliver orders, pay referral commission, prevent fraud and misuse, answer your support requests, and send you notifications about your account.

Who can see it

Only authorised company staff and the agents who process your payment can see what they need for that task (for example, the payment details of a deposit or withdrawal). We do not sell your personal data. We use trusted service providers to host and run the app (for example, database hosting and Telegram for messages). We may share information when the law requires it or to protect against fraud.

Security

We use access controls and encryption in transit, and staff accounts need two-step verification. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and never share verification codes.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep records as long as needed to run your account and to meet legal and accounting needs. You can ask Support to correct or delete your account data. Some records (such as transactions) may have to be kept.

Changes

We may update this page. The date above shows the latest version. If you have a question, contact Support Center.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 25 October 2026

By creating an account or using APRP Digital you agree to these terms.

1. Your account

You must be 18 or older and give correct information. You are responsible for your account and password. One person may keep one account. We may limit or close an account that breaks these terms or is used for fraud.

2. Wallet and deposits

Your wallet balance is added only after we confirm your deposit. Send money only to the number, account or address shown in the app at the time. Money sent to any other place is not our responsibility. For crypto, send only the coin and network shown. A wrong coin or network can be lost for good, and we cannot recover it. The crypto rate is fixed when you submit your request. You must include your payment screenshot and transaction ID, and a transaction ID can be used only once.

3. Withdrawals

To withdraw you need at least one completed deposit and one product purchase. The minimum amount and any fee are shown before you submit. Check your account number carefully, because a payment sent to the number you entered cannot be taken back by us. The amount is deducted from your balance when you submit and is returned if the request is rejected.

4. Products, delivery and buyback

Products are paid from your wallet balance. If you take delivery, it normally takes 12-15 days and the delivery charge is ৳170. If you choose buyback, the company buys the product back at the price shown on the product after its maturity days (the price you paid plus the company percentage). If you sell back earlier, you receive 60% of the price you paid. Buyback requests are approved by the company. Product details and prices may change; the price at the time you buy is the price you pay.

5. Referrals

A referral counts when your friend signs up with your link. Commission is added after their valid product purchases reach ৳1,000, at the rate set by the company at the time. We may withhold commission from fake or abusive referrals.

6. Agents

Payments for deposits and withdrawals may be handled by company agents. Pay only the numbers shown in the app. If you did not receive a withdrawal, report it in the app so we can check.

7. Not allowed

Fake payments or screenshots, using someone else's account, money laundering, abusing offers or referrals, and any illegal activity. We may reject requests, hold funds while we investigate, suspend accounts and report to the authorities where required.

8. Service and changes

We try to keep the app available but we do not promise it will never stop or have errors. We may change services, limits, fees and these terms. Using the app after a change means you accept it.

9. Contact

Use Support Center for any question or complaint.
